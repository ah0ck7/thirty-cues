Watermark Community Church · Production Team · 2017–2026

Thirty Cues

What nearly ten years on the production team taught me, written for whoever sits in the producer’s chair next.

Tech ArtsVideo Production CoordinatorVideo Content DirectorProduction Director, AuditoriumCreative Director

I joined the Tech Arts team in February 2017. On my third day on staff, Blake sent a few of us a note about not letting the demands of ministry crowd out our time with the Lord. I wrote back that I already felt the tug of responsibilities against time abiding, and that I was stopping. That reply turned out to be the first lesson, and it is still the most important one.

Since then I’ve directed video, produced Sundays, led this team, and written W/OFIs nearly every week since 2022. These are thirty things I learned along the way, said the way I’d want to say them to whoever comes next. Each one rests on what we already believe: our vision, our 10 Markers, our team values, and the Objectives & Values we have handed to new volunteers since 2018.

Team values

  • We must first get from Jesus what we need to minister to others. John 15:5

  • We gladly belong to a collaborative body. We follow our leaders, champion our ministry partners, and rely on our teammates. Ephesians 4:3–6

  • Excellence honors God and inspires people. We learn and experiment. We feel safe to risk and are hungry for feedback. Colossians 3:23–24

  • The joy of the Lord is our strength, and it should also show on our faces. Proverbs 17:22

Principles for success

  • Fight hard to maintain unity in pursuit of a common goal, and honor relationships. Ephesians 4:1–8

  • Variables change. Understand the vision and the goals, and with self-control find the best path when they do. Philippians 2:14–16

  • Say yes as often as you can. When you need to say no, help find an appropriate compromise. Nehemiah 4:15–17; Acts 17:22–34

  • Put others’ interests above your own. Initiate with new people. Engage everyone with warmth. Philippians 2:3–4

  • Take a day to prayerfully process. If it still lingers Monday, go start the conversation. Hebrews 3:13; Ephesians 4:26–27; Matthew 18:15; Matthew 5:23–24

Our objective

Our purpose is to call all people to be fully devoted followers of Christ, and our objective is to use our God-given gifts intentionally and with excellence to:

  • Environments where the Body of Christ can gather for worship and teaching.

  • With the communicator, to amplify the gospel, and with others, for a common goal.

  • Our faith and our skills, developing ourselves and others.

  • The Good News, so it encourages people long after it was first shared.

Creative mission and our training

  • Reflecting our Creator, we make things that point to Jesus and build up the Church.

  • Abiding in Jesus, we are making disciples together.

Part one · Cues 01–10

Abiding in Jesus,

Who we are before we touch a fader.

Gospel-Saturated · Praying · Spirit-Led · Bible-Revering

Cue01

Stop and abide. The work will still be there.

The tug I felt on day three never went away. There is always one more render, one more email, one more thing to fix before Sunday. Go to Jesus first anyway. Everything we make flows out of that relationship and runs on the strength He gives. If you skip abiding to get ahead, you end up producing church instead of being part of it.

“Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.”

John 15:5 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itMy reply to Blake’s “Abide Daily” email on February 17, 2017, my third day on staff.

Cue02

Sundays are won on our knees before they’re won at the console.

In August 2023 I wrote in our W/OFIs that Sundays are a result of being a praying people, and that I was at a loss for all the wins. Prayer is built into our rhythm on purpose. Our Tuesday review opens by praying over what God did last weekend and what He will do in the next one. Fifteen minutes before each service we circle up in the green room and pray for the service and for each other. Guard those minutes, especially on the mornings when the gear is fighting you.

“Unless the LORD builds the house, those who build it labor in vain.”

Psalm 127:1 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itW/OFIs, August 20, 2023. Our Sunday rhythm, written up for another church in November 2022. Green room prayer in our 2025 WOFIs.

Cue03

Make room for the Spirit to move, and be ready when He does.

Some of my favorite Sundays didn’t go to plan. When a worship leader feels free to lead what they believe is right, and a teacher feels free to teach what they believe they need to teach, it is usually because production quietly updated the plan and flexed. In February 2024 we prepared a moment with prayer and intentionality, held it loosely, and everyone showed up on the same page. That was Spirit-led. Our readiness is what gives other people the freedom to follow.

“The heart of man plans his way, but the LORD establishes his steps.”

Proverbs 16:9 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itW/OFIs, February 4, 2024. WOFIs, April 27, 2025. “I love when we get to go wherever in the music set,” August 2024.

Cue04

Handle the Word like it’s the Word.

We put Scripture on very large screens in front of thousands of people. A capitalized “Until” in Psalm 71:18, a stray line break in a lower third, the question of “Seven or 7?” are small to a computer and not small to me. We made sentence case for points our standard for a reason. God’s Word deserves the most careful slide we build all week. Proof it, then ask someone else to proof it.

“All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness.”

2 Timothy 3:16 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itW/OFIs, January 2024 and August 2023, when sentence case became our standard. DG/DB, December 2022.

Cue05

Measure Sunday by the conversations down front.

After Christmas Eve 2023, five services in all, the first thing I wanted to tell people was that every service went smooth, the team had a blast, and there were lots of gospel conversations down front afterward. Smooth services and a happy team are gifts. The reason we do any of this is that people would hear the gospel and grow up in Christ. Let that be the scoreboard you care about most.

“Him we proclaim, warning everyone and teaching everyone with all wisdom, that we may present everyone mature in Christ.”

Colossians 1:28 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itMy email after Christmas Eve, December 26, 2023. W/OFIs, August 2024: “Church. The Blood. The message.”

Cue06

Serve the moment. Don’t steal it.

The best looks I’ve seen were barely noticed. Lighting and LED shifted so subtly that the music could lead. Looks changed when the band changed keys, because that was the moment. A camera frame should work as an extension of the worship leader. Before you add anything, ask what the communicator is trying to say and whether this helps them say it. Our creative mission is to make things that point to Jesus. If people leave talking about the lights, we pointed at the wrong thing.

“He must increase, but I must decrease.”

John 3:30 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itDG/DB, October 10, 2022. W/OFIs, September 2024. Switcher training, January 2021. Coaching a communicator on photos in August 2019: “What are you trying to communicate?”

Cue07

The message is for the booth, too.

It is easy to hear a sermon as a list of cues: lower third, slide, camera two. Some of the most faithful people I’ve served with worship while they work. I’ve watched a stage manager worship between calls, a safety volunteer bow their head in prayer with the congregation, and a director write personal takeaways into the production notes. I’ve had messages land so hard they went on repeat for me. Don’t let the booth turn into a bunker. The Word is for you too.

“But be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves.”

James 1:22 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itW/OFIs, October 2, 2024: “The message will be on repeat for me.” W/OFIs, July 2024. WOFIs, June 2025.

Cue08

Do the unseen work as worship, and honor the people who do it.

My first Saturday on staff, I was cleaning lumber out of back of house. Most of production looks like that: swapping out expired communion cups, finding a bulb that matches the others, walking the room with a trash bag, quietly fixing five problems nobody in the room ever knows about. God sees all of it. So say thank you out loud to the audio team that is easy to take for granted, to facilities for the unclogged green room sink, and to the volunteers who bring breakfast.

“Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men.”

Colossians 3:23 (ESV); see also Matthew 10:42
Rooted in

Where I learned itFebruary 18, 2017, back of house. Blake’s “Ordinary faithfulness in the smallest tasks,” August 2019. W/OFIs, November 2023 and July–August 2024. WOFIs, 2025.

Cue09

Joy is part of the job.

One of my favorite notes said run-through was light and fun, with healthy communication between production and worship, like a sports team. Laughing together is normal here. I have bragged about running lyrics from my iPad. I have been unreasonably happy about writing my initials in a shared doc. We officially cleared TA’s white shirt for the rotation. A tense booth makes a tense room, and a joyful team serves longer and better. You can do great work and have a great time doing it.

“For the joy of the LORD is your strength.”

Nehemiah 8:10 (ESV); see also Proverbs 17:22
Rooted in

Where I learned itDG/DB, January 30, 2023. W/OFIs, February 2024. WOFIs, May and June 2025.

Cue10

Remember who the real enemy is.

When a plug-in freezes two weeks in a row, or a change lands at 8:06 AM, it is tempting to aim the frustration at whoever is on the other end of comms. In June 2025 I wrote into our WOFIs that we have an enemy who is looking for chances to tank the moment, the day, our spirits, and our relationships. Support each other and be patient. The person on headset is your teammate. Fight for that relationship.

“For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood.”

Ephesians 6:12 (ESV); see also 1 Peter 5:8
Rooted in

Where I learned itWOFIs, June 1, 2025. W/OFIs, September 2024.

Cue11

People over projects, every Sunday.

We put it right in our training outline: people over projects. Services end. The people we serve with are eternal. I’ve learned to pray with volunteers walking through hard things at home, to say “totally understand, be free” when someone has to drop, and to promise there is never any pressure to say yes. Keep asking how well you know your volunteers, and reach out before Sunday morning. Every so often, sit in their seat and run camera or lyrics yourself.

“In humility count others more significant than yourselves.”

Philippians 2:3 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itSwitcher training, January 2021. Emails to volunteers, 2018–2019. W/OFIs, August and September 2024: “How well do we know our volunteers?”

Cue12

Teach the why before the buttons.

Our switcher training started with purpose and posture before it ever got to shot types or what each button does. New volunteers sat through an Objectives & Values class before they ran anything. After one class, a volunteer wrote back that our values challenged them to be a better employee at work too. That’s the goal. Anyone can learn the buttons on a Sunday. Culture has to be handed down on purpose, and it follows people into their Monday.

“Teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you.”

Matthew 28:20 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itObjectives & Values class, February 2020. Switcher training, January 2021. Shadow onboarding, September 2022. Refreshing the one-pager for onboarding, June 2024.

Cue13

Throw them in. Never throw them under.

People grow when the shot is live. Let operators feel the pressure of their shot going out, because even if a bad one goes out, they will learn. Don’t let a trainee sit unused for two songs. Set people up before you put them in, though. I once watched a lyrics op go solo for the first time without knowing they would be alone, and they weren’t ready. That one was on us. Give real reps, clear expectations, and someone on comms who has their back.

“For if they fall, one will lift up his fellow.”

Ecclesiastes 4:10 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itSwitcher training, January 2021. DG/DB, December 11, 2022. W/OFIs, August 2024, when a new lyrics op shadowed in the morning and ran the 4 PM alone.

Cue14

Encourage during the show. You may not get the chance after.

This came straight from our camera training: encourage your operators while you are live, because you likely won’t get a chance afterward. A quick “great shot, three” on comms lands in the moment and builds trust for the next cue. The same is true in the green room and the hallway. If you are grateful for someone, say it today.

“But exhort one another every day, as long as it is called ‘today.’”

Hebrews 3:13 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itSwitcher training, January 2021. WOFIs, 2025: “Way. To. Go.”

Cue15

If it only works when I’m there, it isn’t finished.

One of the best wins I ever wrote down was that our volunteers ran the entire rehearsal without me. That is the job. Train the next person, write the process down, name files so anyone can find them quickly, keep the stage manager sheets current, and let someone else draft the response and get the credit. Aim to multiply yourself.

“Entrust to faithful men, who will be able to teach others also.”

2 Timothy 2:2 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itMy notes from the Uncommon weekend, November 2022. DG/DB, December 2022. W/OFIs, July 2024. The Loft PA project, June 2026.

Cue16

Safe to try, quick to tweak.

In 2022 I thanked our elders and teaching pastors for an “it’s safe to try” mentality. That posture produced some of our best work: five vocalists on a Sunday, playing the opener, new tools for Easter. At The Porch in 2024 we celebrated that we discussed, we tried, and the things we tried weren’t terrible. Then we made 2% adjustments. Try things, debrief honestly, adjust, and never punish the attempt.

“He who observes the wind will not sow, and he who regards the clouds will not reap.”

Ecclesiastes 11:4 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itDG/DB, October 10, 2022. W/OFIs, December 2023 and January 2024. The Porch W/OFIs, October 3, 2024.

Cue17

Wins first, then the 2%.

In 2023 we renamed “Did Goods / Do Betters” to “Wins / Opportunities for Improvement,” and the order matters. Start with what God did and what the team did well. Some weeks it got hard to name new wins because we had come so far, and I didn’t want us to forget that. Then talk about the last 2% we rarely get to fix during a weekend. As one note in our WOFIs doc put it, the list is there to make us better and to celebrate what we do great. It is never meant to create fear.

“And let us consider how to stir up one another to love and good works.”

Hebrews 10:24 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itRenaming DG/DB, January 31, 2023. W/OFIs, July 22 and September 17, 2024. WOFIs, April 2025.

Cue18

Own the miss first.

Plenty of my OFIs were about me. I forgot to remind people to sit at both services. I forgot we had agreed on IMAG off. I should have spent more time with TA figuring out an announcement moment. Back in 2018, an edit I made during playback caused a hiccup on screen. When the leader names their own miss first, it becomes safe for everyone else to name theirs, and the fix happens faster.

“Whoever conceals his transgressions will not prosper, but he who confesses and forsakes them will obtain mercy.”

Proverbs 28:13 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itThe year-end giving video, December 2018. W/OFIs, March 2023 and October 2024. WOFIs, June 2025.

Cue19

Make it for the person who isn’t in the room.

Our training said it plainly: our job is to give people who aren’t in the room the most engaging experience possible. That might be a family in overflow, a guest in the Town Center, someone watching from home, or someone who finds a message years from now. Some messages deserve to be pinned on the website and played in new member classes. We circulate the good news so it keeps encouraging people long after it was first shared.

“Tell to the coming generation the glorious deeds of the LORD.”

Psalm 78:4 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itSwitcher training, January 2021. W/OFIs, August 2024. Overflow and Town Center notes, 2024–2025.

Cue20

Share the playbook.

For years, churches have asked how we run a Sunday, how we plan CLC, or how to step into a head-of-production role. I’ve learned to answer generously: send the schedule, share the process, offer the job description, set up a lunch. Be honest that we are still figuring a lot of it out. Learn just as humbly from them. We have visited other churches to see their systems and come home better. It is one Kingdom.

“I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth.”

1 Corinthians 3:6 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itEmails with other churches, 2020–2026, including our Sunday rhythm in November 2022 and CLC planning questions in May 2022. Benchmarking visits, 2026.

Part three · Cues 21–30

together.

How we live and lead as one team.

Shepherding · Community · Unifying

Cue21

No one is the primary leader. We all move together.

I wrote that to our lyrics ops in 2025: listen to MD talkback as a guide, because no one is the primary leader and we all move together. It is true of the whole room. We have thanked the band for being teammates in moments that could easily have been frustrating. Teaching, Comms, Production, and Worship have pulled off weekends with flawless communication. One Sunday our video team was a high schooler, a corporate lawyer, a programmer, and a schoolteacher. One body, many parts.

“For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, though many, are one body, so it is with Christ.”

1 Corinthians 12:12 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itWOFIs, April, May, and June 2025. DG/DB, January 30, 2023.

Cue22

Clarity is kindness.

Most of the Sunday stress I’ve seen traces back to someone not knowing something. Changes made in a side conversation turn into a telephone game, so loop in the producer. A pastor who calls in the morning to process and texts to confirm the order gives the whole team confidence. Go get the vision early, too, because the people we serve won’t always say what they are picturing unless you ask. We once learned about an opening illustration at 6 AM on a Sunday and pulled it off, and I still wished we had known sooner. Know who owns what. Sunday works when the right people are in the right roles. Clarity is kindness. Don’t forget to be kind while being clear.

“For God is not a God of confusion but of peace.”

1 Corinthians 14:33 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itPre-production planning notes, May 2022. DG/DB, October 10, 2022. W/OFIs, April 2023 and October 2024. WOFIs, May 2025: “We built the wall on Sunday, with the right people in the necessary role.”

Cue23

Thursday’s prep buys Sunday’s freedom.

Songs in PCO by noon Thursday. Lyrics, bumper, graphics, and lighting reviewed by the end of the day Thursday. A full run-through on Sunday morning as though it’s live. Run the band through what they would do off click, without ears. Practice communion transitions like it’s the real thing. In July 2024 I wrote that our planning and prep were so dialed that it was easy to be flexible, and that I didn’t want to forget it. Plan where you can plan, and stay flexible where you need to.

“The plans of the diligent lead surely to abundance, but everyone who is hasty comes only to poverty.”

Proverbs 21:5 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itOur Sunday rhythm, November 2022. W/OFIs, July 22, 2024. WOFIs, June 2025.

Cue24

“How can I help?” beats “not my problem.”

That is a reminder I wrote to myself in our WOFIs. Sunday has no department lines. I’ve watched an audio engineer get pulled to run content at 8:06 AM, a director take an Uber to church at 5 AM because their car was blocked in, a camera op jump to where the bigger need was, and a teammate call IHOP when a breakfast volunteer canceled. Say yes as often as you can. When you have to say no, help find the next-best answer.

“Bear one another’s burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ.”

Galatians 6:2 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itWOFIs, June 2025. My notes from the Uncommon weekend, November 2022. W/OFIs, February 2024. WOFIs, April 2025.

Cue25

Be a host, not a guest, down to the breakfast table.

Hospitality is ministry. Take the food out of the bags and lay it out with intention. Ask a guest speaker whether family is coming and whether they would like seats saved. Clean up after yourself instead of leaving it for the ASM. Reset the room for the next team, and introduce yourself to new people. The green room shapes how speakers, musicians, and volunteers feel about serving here. It is familial by nature, so keep it that way.

“Show hospitality to one another without grumbling.”

1 Peter 4:9 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itWOFIs, May and June 2025. Our guest speaker hosting document, 2026. W/OFIs, August 2024.

Cue26

Sleep on it, pray on it, then go talk.

Monday conversations is one of my favorite things we believe. If something frustrates you on Sunday, take a day and process it in prayer. If it is still there on Monday, go to the person directly. I’ve had seasons where I carried a tension too long instead of bringing it to the people who could help, and it cost me more than the conversation would have. Ambiguity doesn’t fix itself. Bring it up early and kindly.

“Do not let the sun go down on your anger, and give no opportunity to the devil.”

Ephesians 4:26–27 (ESV); see also Matthew 5:23–24
Rooted in

Where I learned itOur team values since 2017. Conversations with my leaders, 2026.

Cue27

Hold your ideas loosely and your people tightly.

Creative work in a church is shared work. Sometimes a version of your idea makes it into the room instead of the original, and learning to be genuinely proud of that version is part of growing as a leader. When a ministry has a vision for how it is represented, help them feel proud of the result and follow their lead. I’ve had to admit others were right about a response song, and a reworked hero graphic turned out to be a good compromise. Champion other people’s ideas like they are your own.

“Outdo one another in showing honor.”

Romans 12:10 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itW/OFIs, August 2023. Creative direction notes to ministry partners, May 2026. Feedback I’ve written for leaders I care about, 2026.

Cue28

Your voice on comms is the most important piece of gear.

When something breaks live, the room takes its cue from whoever is talking on headset. When the plug-ins froze one Sunday, Blake’s response was “technology isn’t perfect,” and the whole team exhaled. A team that does a little extra work with a calm disposition and a positive attitude can absorb almost anything. Our training put it simply: patience, encouragement, and clarity are key. Keep it calm, keep it short, and keep it kind.

“A soft answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.”

Proverbs 15:1 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itW/OFIs, September 2024 and January 2024. Switcher training, January 2021.

Cue29

Let your team see how you’re actually doing.

In 2024 I was challenged to share how I feel and to pay attention to how I come across with the team. That weekend I tried, and I wrote it into our W/OFIs so I would keep trying. Some years in this role felt like drinking from a fire hydrant. Your team can’t follow a leader they can’t read. Trust grows on three sides, relational, professional, and spiritual. Lead yourself first, ask for help, and let people in.

“My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.”

2 Corinthians 12:9 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itW/OFIs, August 20, 2024. My first years as Creative Director, 2025–2026. Switcher training, January 2021.

Cue30

Guard the team’s Sabbath so they can keep serving with joy.

Our auditorium team takes Friday and Saturday off for a reason. Sunday call times start around 6:30 AM, and big weekends stack up fast. I’ve seen what it costs when people work a full conference and then a full Sunday, and I’ve seen our elders encourage a teaching pastor to take a Sunday off. We can be all in on a vision and still be clear about what our team will carry. Don’t make the producer also own breakfast. Protect your people’s rest so they can keep saying yes for years.

“Come away by yourselves to a desolate place and rest a while.”

Mark 6:31 (ESV)
Rooted in

Where I learned itOur Sunday rhythm, November 2022. My notes from the Uncommon weekend, November 2022. DG/DB, December 2022. Serving as a venue for Gather25, February 2025.

Pass it on

For whoever comes next

Our W/OFIs started as a weekly email and turned into a shared document where everyone signs their initials. I hope this list works the same way. If you are new to the production team, read one of these each week before Sunday. If you lead, push back on them, add your own, and pass them on.

Abiding in Jesus, we are making disciples together.
